Request Executive Briefing
Paid executive diagnostic

Find where agentic AI can create the greatest economic value in your company.

The Agentic Value Assessment maps priority workflows, quantifies the opportunity, evaluates feasibility and risk and defines a governed path to production.

This is not a free AI audit.

It is a four- to six-week board-level engagement that produces a standalone strategic and financial asset. The assessment is designed for companies that have executive sponsorship, a material business problem and the ability to fund implementation if the evidence supports it.

Assessment stages

Six stages, one decision.

01
AlignExecutive interviews, business priorities, constraints and risk appetite → Agreed decision criteria and scope.
02
MapWorkflow, system, data, role, handoff and exception inventory → Current-state workflow and bottleneck map.
03
QuantifyBaseline cost, revenue, volume, quality and cycle time → Conservative, expected and expansion cases.
04
DesignHuman-agent model, authority, architecture and governance → Target operating design and technical approach.
05
PrioritizeValue, feasibility, risk and implementation scoring → Prioritized portfolio and recommended pilot.
06
DecideExecutive review and board-ready presentation → Fund, revise or stop decision.

Gates, not dates: every stage produces evidence you can check, not a date on a calendar. We don't move to the next stage on schedule — we move when the evidence clears the gate.

Fit criteria

Who this is designed for.

  • Company generally has at least $25 million in revenue or a comparably complex operating environment.
  • A senior executive will sponsor the work and provide access to functional leaders.
  • The company can share enough operating data to establish a baseline.
  • There is a defined revenue, cost, capacity or speed problem with material economic value.
  • Leadership is prepared to fund a pilot if the assessment supports it.
