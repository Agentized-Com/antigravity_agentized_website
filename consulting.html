How we measure value

Every engagement is measured against six questions.

  1. Did it generate or protect revenue?
  2. Did it reduce the cost of producing the outcome?
  3. Did it increase throughput or capacity?
  4. Did it compress cycle time?
  5. Did it maintain or improve quality?
  6. Did employees and customers adopt it?

A favorable estimate becomes credible only when production results appear in operating measures and financial outcomes.

The standard

We report measured impact, not estimates.

Every Agentized engagement begins with a baseline and ends with an evidence review. We measure revenue, margin, capacity, speed, quality and adoption, then compare what we projected against what actually happened.

A projection is not a result. It becomes one only when it shows up in your operating numbers and your financials.

FAQ

Agentic AI consulting questions, answered.

What does Agentized do?

Agentized helps mid-market companies redesign high-value revenue and operating workflows around governed AI agents. It combines strategy, workflow redesign, implementation, governance and change management to move agentic systems from executive priority to production.

How does an Agentized engagement start?

Agentized engagements begin with a paid Agentic Value Assessment, which takes four to six weeks. Each offer leads to the next.

What engagements does Agentized offer?

Agentic Value Assessment (4–6 weeks); 90-Day Agentic Value Pilot (12–14 weeks, one to three production workflows); Enterprise Agentization Program (6–18 months); Managed Agentic Operations (12-month minimum); and annual Board and CEO Advisory.

What is the Agentized Value System?

The Agentized Value System is five steps from a business opportunity to an agent running in production: Diagnose, Redesign, Deploy, Prove and Scale.

How does Agentized measure the value of AI agents?

Every engagement is measured against six questions: did it generate or protect revenue, reduce the cost of producing the outcome, increase throughput or capacity, compress cycle time, maintain or improve quality, and get adopted by employees and customers.

Why do most AI programs stall?

Most AI programs do not fail because the model is weak. They stall because the company selected a low-value task, preserved a broken workflow, ignored integration, never established a financial baseline, failed to assign an owner or treated governance and adoption as work to complete later.

How do I choose an AI consulting firm as a mid-size company?

Start with one manual internal workflow, set a measurable baseline, and choose the firm that can integrate with your existing systems and gives a scoped price. Agentized compares AI consulting firms for mid-size companies in Best AI Consulting Firms for Mid-Size Companies.

How do you implement AI in a mid-size company's operations?

Pick one high-value operational workflow, set a financial baseline, rebuild the workflow around human judgment and agent execution, then deploy and measure it. Agentized does this through a 4–6 week Agentic Value Assessment followed by a 12–14 week pilot. See AI consulting for mid-size companies.

Is Agentized a fit for small businesses?

Agentized is designed for companies with roughly $25 million or more in revenue. Smaller businesses can start with free help from SCORE and their local Small Business Development Center. Agentized's small business guide compares other options.

How much does AI implementation cost for a mid-size company?

Does Agentized work with our existing systems?

Yes. Agents are built, configured, integrated and evaluated in a controlled production environment connected to your existing systems and data.
