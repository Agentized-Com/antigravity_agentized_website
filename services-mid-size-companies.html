AI Consulting for Mid-Size Companies

Agentized helps mid-size companies replace manual, internal operational work with governed AI agents. We find the workflow where AI creates the most economic value, rebuild it around your people and your existing systems, put it into production, and measure the result against a financial baseline.

Agentized was founded by Jas Dhillon and Mark Fidelman, authors of the upcoming book AGENT SHOCK: Surviving and Profiting from the Rise of AI Agents and Robots. The book lays out the thinking behind how we work, and you can read the introduction now.

Who this is for

Agentized works with mid-size companies that generally have:

  • At least $25 million in revenue, or a comparably complex operating environment.
  • A senior executive who will sponsor the work and give access to functional leaders.
  • Enough operating data to establish a baseline.
  • A defined revenue, cost, capacity or speed problem with material economic value.
  • Leadership prepared to fund a pilot if the evidence supports it.

What we do for mid-size companies

Find the right workflow. Most mid-size AI projects stall because the company picked a low-value task, kept a broken workflow, ignored integration, never set a financial baseline, had no owner, or left governance and adoption for later. We start by mapping your value chain, systems, data, bottlenecks and economics, and choose where the case is strongest.

Redesign the work, not just add a tool. We rebuild the priority workflow around human judgment, agent execution and explicit decision rights, so it is clear what the agent does, what people approve and who owns the outcome.

Integrate with the systems you already run. We build, configure, integrate, evaluate and launch agents in a controlled production environment connected to your existing systems and data.

Measure it. We measure the result against the baseline, including operating and technology costs, and expand only what works.

What it costs

The Agentic Value Assessment is a paid engagement. It is not a free AI audit. Pricing for the assessment and pilot is on request.

How we measure results

Every engagement is measured against six questions:

  1. Did it generate or protect revenue?
  2. Did it reduce the cost of producing the outcome?
  3. Did it increase throughput or capacity?
  4. Did it compress cycle time?
  5. Did it maintain or improve quality?
  6. Did employees and customers adopt it?
