Agentized Command

One screen that shows every agent your company runs, what it costs, and who owns it.

Agentized Command is the proposed value, governance and operating layer for organizations deploying agents across multiple workflows, systems, models and vendors.

It is being developed through selected client engagements and design partnerships, with an initial focus on ownership, authority, performance, cost, risk and business value.

Design concept — illustrative data, not production client data

Why leaders need this

As companies deploy more agents, ownership gets harder to see.

As companies deploy more agents across more systems, leaders need a clear view of what those agents are doing, what they cost, who is accountable and whether they are producing business value.

AGENTIZED! calls each agent's file its Boat Papers. Agentized Command is where every boat's papers live in one place, instead of scattered across whichever team stood the agent up.

Executive portfolioCross-workflow value, risk, ownership and decision queue.

Each module is being validated through client engagements and design partnerships before it is presented as generally available.

What Agentized Command is not

It doesn't replace your agent builders, automation platforms or enterprise applications.

It connects them. One view shows which business workflow each agent serves, who owns the outcome, what authority the agent has, what it costs, and whether the result reached the P&L.

Building toward the Command layer with a small number of partners.

Design partnerships are selected alongside Agentic Value Assessment and pilot engagements.
