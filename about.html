Why Agentized exists

The gap between AI experimentation and operating results.

Most companies now have access to capable AI models. Very few have redesigned the workflows, decision rights, governance and incentives around them. The book calls that disorientation AgentShock — the moment the water reaches a rung your organization thought was safe. Agentized exists to close the gap it exposes: combining executive strategy, workflow engineering, implementation, governance, software and ongoing operations into one accountable partner, rather than leaving companies to assemble strategy, tooling and governance from separate vendors.
