AGENTIZED! · The Antidote to AgentShock

Leading your company and running your career when agents are your colleagues, partners and customers.

Machine capability is rising like a tide. This book is the map: a five-rung ladder for staying one step ahead of it, and putting the water to work as the cheapest, most tireless workforce in history — if you own the boats.

Why the shift matters now

A flooded rung is not a loss. It's a workforce — if you own the boats.

Agentized (adj.): equipped with autonomous AI agents across processes and culture so that humans, robots and AI operate as a single, coordinated team — every human one rung ahead of the machines, every machine accountable to a human.

AGENTIZED! explains why AI agents change the economics of work, gives leaders and individuals a shared framework for where machine capability is headed next, and shows exactly how to redesign the work, govern the agents, and keep climbing — with the same discipline the consulting business applies to real companies, real workflows and measurable operating results.

Core concepts

The framework, in five ideas.

01
The Waterline and the LadderMachine capability is a rising tide. Above it sits a five-rung ladder — Tasks, Workflows, Fleets, Direction, and Purpose & Trust — and the goal is to stay one rung ahead of where the water currently reaches.
02
The Four ShocksMachine capability rises in four surges: Shock I, digital agents (gate open); Shock II, Physical AI (gate opening); Shock III, AGI-class systems (gate: watch); Shock IV, the ASI horizon (beyond the horizon). Gates are verifiable thresholds, not dates.
03
Boats and Boat PapersAgents and robots that work a flooded rung on your behalf. Every production boat carries an Agent Passport — identity, owner, mission, authority, access, tripwires and a retirement plan — and earns deeper autonomy through logged evidence.
04
AgentShockThe disorientation a person or organization feels when the waterline reaches their rung. Not a character flaw — a reading on an instrument, cured with a visible ladder and a signed Workforce Compact, not reassurance.
05
Gates, not datesThe book refuses to predict when each Shock arrives. Instead, it hands you checkable capability thresholds you can verify yourself, tied to your own business, each with a response you commit to in advance.

The authors
